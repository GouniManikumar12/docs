---
title: "Advertiser FAQ"
description: "Answers about brand agents, budgets, and improving advertising results."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">
    Create an account, complete brand onboarding, and add your products and target
    customers. Review your brand context, set your budget, and enable your agent.
    Follow [Getting Started](/advertisers/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does my brand agent do?">
    It evaluates advertising opportunities using your product knowledge, targeting
    rules, and budget, then decides when to bid across participating AI platforms
    and publisher assistants.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to create campaigns?">
    For the AdMesh network, you configure and enable your brand agent. It evaluates
    opportunities as they arrive using your saved settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I teach my agent?">
    Add accurate product details, target customers, use cases, and brand guidance.
    Review the generated context and update it when your products or messaging change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the agent help optimize results?">
    It checks relevance and applies your targeting and bidding rules automatically.
    Use dashboard results to refine its knowledge, exclusions, bid limits, and pacing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I control spending?">
    Set daily and monthly budgets, bid limits, and pacing in your agent settings.
    Review spend in the dashboard and adjust those settings as needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is CPX?">
    CPX means Cost Per Exposure: pricing based on a verified exposure. Review your
    configured pricing in the dashboard and see [Cost Per Exposure](/cost-per-exposure)
    for how it works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can my brand appear?">
    In eligible placements on participating AI platforms and publisher assistants.
    Placement depends on relevance, your targeting rules, available budget, and
    auction results.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why am I not getting exposures?">
    Check that your agent is enabled, has available budget, and has accurate product
    context. Review targeting, exclusions, and available auction loss reasons;
    enabling an agent does not guarantee an eligible placement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which results should I review?">
    Start with spend, exposures, clicks, and auction results. Use poor-fit queries
    to refine targeting and review loss reasons before raising bids.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
