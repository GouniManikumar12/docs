---
title: "Getting Started"
description: "Set up a brand agent to find relevant advertising opportunities and manage participation within your budget."
---

Your brand agent uses your products, audience, and brand guidelines to decide
which advertising opportunities fit your business. Once enabled, it evaluates
opportunities as they arrive across participating AI platforms and publisher
assistants, using your targeting rules and spending limits.

## 1. Create your brand agent

[Create an account](https://useadmesh.com/auth/signin/?role=brand&type=get-started&mode=signup)
and complete brand onboarding in the dashboard.

Add your website, products, target customers, and key use cases. Review the
generated brand context and correct anything the agent should know before it
represents you.

## 2. Set your budget and rules

Choose your daily and monthly budget, bid limits, target locations, and allowed
formats. Add exclusions for queries or topics where your brand should not appear.

Enable your agent when its context and settings are ready.

## How your agent helps optimize advertising

| What it does | How it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Checks conversation relevance | Evaluates whether your product fits what the user is asking for. |
| Decides whether to bid | Filters opportunities using your brand context, targeting, and exclusions. |
| Uses your pricing and budget settings | Keeps participation tied to the spending rules you set. |
| Uses shared brand knowledge | Guides how your products are represented in relevant conversations. |

For example, a project-management brand can describe its focus on remote teams
and exclude personal task-list queries. Those details help the agent distinguish
relevant team-software requests from queries outside its audience.

## 3. Review results and improve your agent

Use the dashboard to review spend, exposures, clicks, and auction results.

- **Poor-fit opportunities:** refine your target customers, use cases, and exclusions.
- **Inaccurate messaging:** update product details and brand guidance.
- **Relevant opportunities lost:** review the available loss reasons before changing bid limits.
- **Spend needs adjusting:** change your budget or pacing settings based on results.

Your agent handles opportunity evaluation automatically. You guide improvements
by keeping its knowledge current and adjusting its settings as you learn.
