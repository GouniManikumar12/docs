---
title: "Contextual Relevance Score (CRS)"
description: "How AdMesh measures whether a recommendation fits the conversation"
---

**CRS** is a 0–100 score for how well a product fits a user’s question.

AdMesh uses it to show recommendations only when they belong in the conversation.

## Score ranges

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **90–100** | Excellent match — shown first |
| **75–89** | Strong fit — shown often |
| **60–74** | Fair fit — shown when space allows |
| **45–59** | Weak fit — limited visibility |
| **0–44** | Too weak — not shown |

Aim for **75+**.

## What affects CRS

- How closely your product matches the query
- Category and description quality
- Listing clarity and completeness
- Brand trust on the network
- Competitiveness of your offer

## How to improve it

1. Write clear descriptions of what you offer and who it’s for.
2. Use specific categories (e.g. “CRM for small teams,” not “Business tools”).
3. Use language your customers actually search with.
4. Keep listings accurate and up to date.
