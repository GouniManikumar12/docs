---
title: "CPM vs CPX"
description: "Why AdMesh uses CPX instead of CPM for AI conversations"
---

**CPM** prices ads per 1,000 page impressions.  
**CPX** prices ads per verified exposure inside an AI answer.

CPM assumes a page, banner, or feed. AI chats don’t have those — so AdMesh uses CPX.

## Side by side

| | CPM | CPX |
|---|-----|-----|
| **Measures** | Page impressions | Verified show in an AI response |
| **Based on** | Ad slots / banners | User intent and relevance |
| **Tracking** | Cookies / viewability | Server-side verification |
| **Best for** | Websites and feeds | AI chats, search, and agents |

## Simple example

**CPM:** User loads a page → banner may appear → you pay, even with no intent.

**CPX:** User asks “best CRM for small teams” → your offer is shown in the answer → you pay for that verified exposure.

## When to use which

- Use **CPX** for AI conversations and intent-led placements.
- Use **CPM** for traditional display and high-volume awareness.
