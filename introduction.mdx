AdMesh connects brands with people asking for products and services inside AI
platforms and publisher assistants.

Brand agents use product knowledge, targeting rules, and budgets to evaluate
advertising opportunities. AI platforms and publishers earn revenue by showing
relevant sponsored recommendations.

## How it works

1. A user asks a question in a participating app or publisher assistant.
2. Brand agents evaluate the opportunity, and AdMesh selects an eligible recommendation.
3. The recommendation appears with an Ad label, and AdMesh tracks exposure and engagement.
